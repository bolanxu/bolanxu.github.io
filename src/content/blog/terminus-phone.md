## The problem

I didn't want another smartphone. I wanted something I could use to communicate without also getting a machine designed to compete for my attention.

That sounds simple until you try to build it.

## The first rule

The device should be useful enough to carry, but constrained enough to stay focused on communication.

That one rule affects almost everything: the display, keyboard, software architecture, enclosure, and even what the device is allowed to do.

## Why build it yourself?

Because making the constraints physical changes the problem. Instead of asking which app should handle a feature, I get to ask whether the feature belongs on the device at all.

That is what makes the project interesting to me.
